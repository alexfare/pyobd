# pyOBD Mobile Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

pyOBD Mobile does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, vehicle data, diagnostic information, usage analytics, or advertising identifiers with Fare Brothers or third parties.

The app stores connection settings, vehicle profiles, PID preferences, and recordings locally on the user's device. This information leaves the device only when the user chooses to export or share it.

pyOBD Mobile uses Bluetooth or the local Wi-Fi network only to communicate with the vehicle adapter selected by the user. The app does not use these connections for tracking or advertising.

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the [pyOBD support page](https://farebros.com/module.php?module=app_directory&page=support&slug=pyobd).
