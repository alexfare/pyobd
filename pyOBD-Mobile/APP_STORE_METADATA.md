## Description

pyOBD Mobile brings practical OBD-II diagnostics and live vehicle data to iPhone.

Connect to a compatible Wi-Fi or Bluetooth Low Energy OBD-II adapter to inspect standard diagnostic information from your vehicle. A built-in Demo mode lets you explore the app without connecting to a vehicle.

Features include:

- Live OBD-II data with selectable PIDs and polling rates
- Multi-PID graphs for monitoring vehicle data over time
- Stored, pending, and permanent diagnostic trouble codes
- Clear descriptions for recognized trouble codes
- Emissions readiness monitor status
- Freeze-frame data
- Vehicle profiles with saved PID preferences
- CSV recording and export
- Wi-Fi and Bluetooth Low Energy adapter support

Vehicle and adapter support varies. pyOBD Mobile focuses on standard OBD-II diagnostics; manufacturer-specific functions may not be available. Always use the app safely and never interact with it while driving.

pyOBD Mobile is the iPhone companion to the pyOBD desktop project, redesigned for a native mobile experience.
